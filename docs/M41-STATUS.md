# M41 — Enterprise GA & Certification

## Status

**Complete** — the repository now has a durable release-assurance surface for release identity, compatibility, evidence-backed checks and deterministic GA readiness.

## Boundary

M41 is an assurance contract, not an external certification. It cannot establish SOC 2, ISO 27001, penetration-test completion or customer-specific compliance by itself. Those require external assessment and deployment evidence.

## Acceptance

- releases require artifact digest, SBOM and provenance references;
- completed checks require evidence references;
- pending or failed checks prevent readiness;
- compatibility records are explicit and durable;
- external assurance is represented as evidence rather than asserted;
- no authority plane is introduced.
