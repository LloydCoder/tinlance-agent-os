# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Tinlance Agent OS project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other characteristic.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to conduct they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **@LloydCoder**, the repository maintainer, through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

All reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not permitted.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will:

1. acknowledge a report when practicable;
2. assess the reported conduct and relevant context;
3. determine an appropriate response proportionate to the circumstances;
4. communicate the decision to involved parties when appropriate;
5. document enforcement decisions privately.
